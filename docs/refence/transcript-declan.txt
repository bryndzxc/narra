REFERENCE TRANSCRIPT — "Declan / Aurelia"
Runtime: ~32:30. Fifteen announced chapters.
Genre: comedy, not betrayal. The engine is a fantasy premise — she can hear his
thoughts — and every laugh comes from the gap between his polite outer voice and
his mercenary inner one.
Use: the thinks-versus-says device only. Do not merge this premise with the
betrayal spine.

---

0:08 I get paid a small fortune to be the perfect assistant to Aurelia Blackwood, the tech CEO the press had nicknamed the ice queen of Larks Valley. Outwardly, I nodded. I smiled. I agreed with everything she said. Inwardly, I mentally billed her for emotional damages every single time she used the word synergy.
0:25 It was a flawless arrangement until yesterday when I was quietly plotting to steal a $200 pen off her desk and she burst out laughing in the middle of a board meeting.
0:34 My billionaire boss could apparently read my mind and instead of firing me on the spot, she gave me a massive raise.
0:42 Hour four in the glass boardroom. My feet ached, my smile had gone stiff and the air conditioning was cold enough that I'd lost feeling in my fingers.
0:50 I stood exactly two steps behind the executive chair because that was my job. Hand her folders, agree with everything she said, and make it look effortless.
1:07 "We need to streamline the acquisition," she told the room, her voice flat and cold. "If we merge the server infrastructure by Q3, we create immediate cross-platform synergy."
1:23 I stepped forward and handed her the next quarterly report. "Flawless and inspiring presentation, Miss Blackwood," I said, my voice smooth and professional.
1:30 Inside my skull, I let out a long silent scream. If she says the word synergy one more time, I am billing her for emotional damages. My brain cells are staging a walkout.
1:44 Aurelia froze mid handoff, the report suspended in the air. Slowly, she turned and looked directly at me. The room went utterly silent.
2:08 Oh no, I thought. She knows about the breakroom coffee. She knows I bought the cheap generic beans and pocketed the $18 difference. I'm going to get fired over instant roast.
2:31 "Meeting adjourned," she said, grabbed her tablet and walked out through the glass doors.

2:46 Chapter 2. We reached the executive suite.
3:02 I would sell a kidney for a double espresso right now, I thought. But if I leave to hit the cafeteria, she'll dock my pay for an unauthorized break. I have to survive on pure greed.
3:16 Without looking up from her screen, Aurelia said, "Declan." "Yes, Ms. Blackwood." "Call the Velvet Bean downstairs. Tell them to send up a double espresso. Make it large."
3:34 Aurelia Blackwood did not buy people coffee.
3:50 This is a trap, I thought wildly. The ice queen doesn't buy coffee out of the goodness of her heart. She's going to deduct this from my paycheck or use the receipt to frame me for corporate embezzlement.
4:16 "Not at all," I said. I am going to pour it directly into the nearest potted plant, I thought. You will not catch me slipping, you magnificent menace.
4:26 Aurelia let out a short, quiet laugh like glass chiming. In two years working for her, I had never once heard her laugh.
4:33 "Make sure you drink every drop," she said softly. "You look like you need it."

4:50 Chapter 3. The espresso arrived 10 minutes later.
5:14 My spine feels like rusted scaffolding, I thought. If I yawn, she fires me. If I blink too long, she fires me.
5:39 "Do you need the Q3 files, Miss Blackwood?" "No," she said, leaning back in her chair. "I need you to go home."
6:00 "Take the afternoon off fully paid."
6:17 Getting sent home early on a Tuesday meant exactly one thing, administrative leave.
6:25 I gave her a perfectly grateful nod. "I appreciate the rest, Miss Blackwood. I'll see you tomorrow."
6:36 I'm being fired, I thought in a full and blinding panic. I need to wipe my browser history and move my savings tonight.
7:05 The moment the glass doors closed behind me, I broke into a dead sprint for the elevators.

7:15 Chapter 4. I didn't sleep. I spent the entire night packing a single suitcase and shredding old grocery receipts, waiting for the police to kick in my door. Nobody came.
7:52 As I reached for the kettle, the cheap metal clasp of my $12 watch snagged hard on my cuff, pulling a thread loose.
7:59 I hate this piece of junk, I thought bitterly. A dress shirt costs $40. If this watch ruins one more of them, I'm throwing it in the river.
8:14 She was already holding out a heavy velvet box across the marble desk.
8:20 Inside, resting on black silk, sat a sleek platinum watch.
8:30 "Your current watch is ruining your cuffs," she said.
8:47 "A beautiful timepiece. Thank you, Miss Blackwood. I'll wear it with pride." This is a tracking device, I thought. Or a bribe. She's handing me a $30,000 asset with no paper trail.
9:17 Aurelia choked on her tea, slamming the cup down and coughing into her napkin, face turning bright red.
9:36 I strapped it on, privately concluding that the golden handcuffs had officially been locked. The game was on.

9:46 Chapter 5. At 1:00 we arrived at Ember and Oak, a steakhouse downtown, to meet Chase Redmond, a rival tech CEO.
10:12 "Does he have to stand right there? It's weird. Tell your glorified bag boy to go wait in the car. Adults are talking."
10:27 "You're absolutely right, sir. I'll step outside." Your stock is down 40%, you cheapskate, I thought. You've got a fake tan and you owe millions in back taxes. Keep talking. My hourly rate just doubled for looking at that tie.
10:49 Aurelia set her water glass down hard enough to cut through the quiet restaurant like a gunshot. "Declan stays right here."
11:06 "Declan, remind HR to raise your base salary by 20% effective immediately."
11:15 "You're too generous, Miss Blackwood. I'll send the email right now." The audit, I thought, panic flooding my chest. She's raising my pay so my income bracket matches the stolen funds. I'm the designated fall guy.
11:53 "Just business, Chase," she said, finally looking up with a huge, terrifying smile. "Just business."

12:02 Chapter 6. I refused to go home. If I went home, they could fire me over the phone.
12:25 I'm going to die here, I thought. My blood is turning into pure caffeine. I miss my cheap mattress.
12:48 "I need you to stop working," she said, and pulled a heavy silver key card from her drawer. "Take the private elevator to the penthouse suite upstairs. Sleep there tonight."
13:19 "The lobby couch is fine, Miss Blackwood," I said quickly. "But I accept your kind offer."
13:28 I'm going to die in a silk bathrobe, I thought wildly. Soundproof walls, a private elevator. It's the perfect place to hide a body.
13:42 Aurelia pressed her lips together, face turning pink, and hid behind a file folder, giggling.

14:08 Chapter 7. I woke up alive. No assassins had come through the vents.
14:15 At 9 sharp, three men in identical gray suits walked into the executive suite. "Financial review team. We're here for the quarterly audit."
14:32 My blood went cold. The pieces fell into place. The $30,000 watch, the sudden 20% raise, the night in the penthouse. She'd built an untraceable paper trail straight to my desk.
14:42 "Of course, gentlemen. We expected you." Then I turned, grabbed my personal grocery receipts from last week, and shoved them into the paper shredder.
15:14 I will flip on her so fast, I thought, bracing for war. Total immunity. I'll draw them a map to her hidden safes. I'll wear two wires. I am not going to prison for a woman who drinks hot water and lemon.
15:39 She let out a loud snort, covering her face. "I'm fine, Declan. Has everything handled." She fled into her private office and slammed the door.

16:07 Chapter 8. The auditors found nothing and packed up by 3.
16:34 This collar feels like sandpaper, I thought. If I get a rash from this fabric, I am billing HR for dermatology.
16:41 Aurelia looked up from her screen and picked up her phone. "Send the tailor up."
16:53 They unzipped it to reveal a midnight blue velvet tuxedo. "Put it on," Aurelia said.
17:18 Don't spill wine on this, I told myself. This suit costs more than my car. When the FBI arrests her tonight and the company folds, I'm taking this home.
17:33 She stepped close and adjusted my bow tie, fighting a grin. "You look very handsome, Declan," she said softly. "But please do not sell my clothes on the internet."
17:41 I froze completely. She's reading my body language, I thought in a panic.

18:10 Chapter 9. The grand ballroom of the Aldermere Hotel smelled like expensive perfume and cold cash.
18:35 "Aurelia," Chase said loudly. "I see you brought the help to the party. Does he fetch your drinks or just stand there looking stupid?"
18:50 "I'm just here to serve, Chase," I said clearly. Joke's on you, buddy, I thought. Your company missed earnings by 3 million last week. Your boat got repossessed. You smell like cheap hairspray. This is time and a half. I'm buying a jet ski with this overtime.
19:19 "Good boy. Now go fetch me a fresh napkin."

19:44 Chapter 10. Her face had gone bright red, both hands pressed over her mouth, unable to hold back a loud, genuine snort. Then the ice queen of Larks Valley completely broke.
19:54 She threw her head back and laughed, a massive booming sound that carried across the suddenly silent ballroom.
20:17 The older board members began whispering, clearly convinced they'd stumbled onto a secret office romance.
20:30 "Is everything all right, boss?" I asked, my voice perfectly steady while my mind screamed. Oh god, she's laughing. This is the signal. She bought me the suit so my corpse looks good for the press photos.
20:49 "You should go home, Chase. Save your money. You can't afford to talk to him."

21:25 Chapter 11. At 7 the next morning I slid a crisp white envelope across the desk.
21:51 "I'm terminating my contract, Miss Blackwood. Keep the signing bonus. I'll waive severance. Just let me go."
22:00 Take the money, I begged silently. Keep the watch. Just don't put out a hit on me.
22:26 "You've been incredibly generous, boss. But I have to move on." You bought me a corpse suit, I thought.
22:50 "I reject your resignation."
23:04 "Declan, stop. You are not going to federal prison. The FBI is not coming for you."
23:11 I froze one foot from the door. I had never said a single word about the FBI out loud.

23:21 Chapter 12. "There is no corporate fraud. I'm not framing you. I'm not putting a hit on you. And I do not want you fleeing to Canada."
23:52 How does she know my escape plan? Did she tap my phone? Put a microphone in the watch. "I did not bug your watch, Declan," she blurted.
24:05 "I hear your thoughts," she said. "It started during the synergy board meeting. You said my presentation was flawless out loud and in your head you threatened to bill me for emotional damages."
24:20 She repeated my thoughts back to me word for word.
24:44 "You hurt me every time. Every single time," she said with a small, hopeful smile. "You don't have to run, Declan. You're safe."
25:14 "I actually like the perfume," she said quietly. "But yes, I know about the coffee fund."
25:21 "I'm not firing you, Declan. In fact, I think we need to renegotiate your contract."

25:37 Chapter 13. I refused to quit. I liked money far too much.
25:45 If she could hear my thoughts, I would simply give her the most boring thoughts imaginable.
26:07 Think about spreadsheets, I commanded myself. Column A, column B. Wait, did I leave that silver pen in my jacket?
26:33 "Declan, I know you took the pen. I don't care about the pen. You make six figures. Buy your own pens."
27:06 "Stop filtering your thoughts. You were just screaming about office supplies in my head. I liked it better when you were insulting my rivals."
27:19 I'm billing her a mental privacy fee, I thought immediately. $50 a day. If she gets to live in my head rent-free, she can pay the utilities.
27:30 "Approved. Add it to your next invoice."

27:47 Chapter 14. Two days later, the magic broke.
28:25 "Declan, what are you thinking right now?" I tested her, thinking as loudly as I could. I'm stealing your desk chair tomorrow.
28:40 "Say something. Why is it so quiet? I can't hear you."
29:03 The connection was gone.
29:14 She dropped her tablet onto the seat and stared out the window, looking for the first time lonely. The ice queen again, cut off from the one person who'd never lied to her.
29:21 I sat forward and dropped the polite mask. "Look," I said aloud. "If you want me to stay, you're going to have to actually talk to me because I'm not leaving until I get my end-of-year bonus."
29:44 "And I want that $50 privacy fee added to my base salary. You just have to hear it out loud now."
30:12 We didn't need the magic anymore. I could just demand her money directly.

30:16 Chapter 15. "Your new contract," she said.
30:41 The first page was a marriage license. The second was a prenuptial agreement.
31:02 Is she using me as a tax shelter? No, this is a hostile takeover.
31:18 "You lost your mind reading powers," I said slowly. "So you decided to buy my brain instead."
31:26 "I'm securing a valuable corporate asset. You're ruthless. You never lie to me, and you care about profit exactly as much as I do. We make a terrifying team. I want a permanent merger."
31:42 She hadn't offered flowers or poetry or empty promises. She'd offered a flawless, unbreakable contract with a spectacular profit margin.
31:57 I'm going to marry a billionaire, I thought happily. I am never buying generic coffee again.
32:04 "I've read the prenup. The terms are acceptable."
32:20 "So, partner," she said softly. "Do we have a deal?" "We have a deal," I said. "But I still want overtime for the weekends."
